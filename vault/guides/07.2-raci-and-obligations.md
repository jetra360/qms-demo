# 07.2 — RACI and obligations

**Question this page answers:** *How do I show who is responsible, accountable, consulted and informed?*

## The pathway

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    O["Obligation"] --> S["Source clause wikilink"]
    S --> R["Responsible"]
    S --> A["Accountable"]
    S --> C["Consulted"]
    S --> I["Informed"]
    R --> D["Due + evidence link"]
    D --> V(["Register answers 'who owes what?'"] )
```

## Analogy

> **Who holds the pen, who signs, who is merely told.** One row makes the difference explicit.

## Why / when / who / where

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why** | Rights and duties need a single auditable list, not folklore. |
| **When** | Contract review, GDPR duties, role changes. |
| **Who** | QA director owns the register. |
| **Where** | `registers/raci-obligations.sheet.md`. |

## Try it

Open the RACI sheet and read the 'Verify CAPA effectiveness' row.

## What you'll see in Tolaria

- Cells contain wikilinks to roles and evidence
- Source clauses are clickable
- The sheet is an index, not the source of truth
