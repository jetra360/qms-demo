# NC-2026-11-19-001 — Damaged seals on incoming material

## Condition

On 2026-11-17, two of twenty sampled units from supplier batch B-2611-17 had damaged tamper seals. Detected during incoming inspection and captured in the Inbox as an untyped note before triage.

## Criteria

[[iso2015-8-7-Control-of-nonconforming-outputs|ISO 9001:2015 §8.7]] and [[SOP-QA-001]] require nonconforming material to be identified and controlled. [[POL-QUAL-001]] requires conforming input.

## Consequence

Compromised seals could allow tampering or contamination to go unnoticed.

## Recommended action

Reject the affected cartons, raise a supplier complaint and verify seal integrity on the next three deliveries. Disposition evidence is recorded in [[REC-NC-2026-11-19-001]].

## Link to CAPA

Escalated to [[CAPA-2026-002]] because the same supplier is already under investigation.
