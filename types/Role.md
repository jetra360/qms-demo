# Role

A responsibility that can be held by different people over time. Owner, approver and verifier fields should point at Roles (or at a Person who currently holds one).

## Purpose

Why does this role exist in the QMS?

## Responsibilities

What the role is accountable for.

## Authority

What this role may approve or sign off.

## Required training

Which SOPs and records are prerequisites for holding the role.
