# CAPA-2026-002 — Supplier defect investigation

## Problem statement

A delivery of raw material contained units outside the specified dimensional tolerance. The defect was found by production, not by incoming inspection, on 2026-11-05.

## Root cause

The supplier's process drifted, and the previous sampling plan (AQL 2.5) had a high probability of missing the defect. Detection failed before prevention was possible.

## Corrective action

- Revised [[SOP-QA-001]] to rev 4 with AQL 1.0 sampling.
- Added the supplier to the monitored list in [[risk-register.sheet]].
- Operator retraining recorded in [[TRAIN-REC-2026-07]].
