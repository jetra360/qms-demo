# CAPA-2026-001 — Overdue review process

## Problem statement

Raised from [[AUDIT-2026-Q1]]: two procedures were past their review date with no notification to the owner.

## Root cause

The review schedule lived in a manually maintained spreadsheet with no automated alert, so a due date could pass unnoticed.

## Corrective action

- Created the saved view `views/overdue-reviews.yml` so overdue documents are visible without manual checking.
- Specified a CI gate that fails an active document with a past `review_due` date (documented in `README.md`; not part of this vault-only deliverable).

## Verification

Verified on 2026-05-20 that the saved view listed the two affected documents and that both were brought under review.
