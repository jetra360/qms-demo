# QA Manager

## Purpose

Owns the document control process and approves procedures.

## Responsibilities

- Approving SOPs and policies.
- Maintaining [[document-master.sheet]] and [[raci-obligations.sheet]].
- Assigning CAPA owners and tracking due dates.

## Authority

May approve [[SOP-QA-001]] and other procedures. May not verify CAPA effectiveness — that belongs to [[qa-director]].
