# QA Engineer

## Purpose

Executes day-to-day quality controls and investigations.

## Responsibilities

- Incoming inspection per [[SOP-QA-001]].
- Nonconformance handling per [[SOP-QA-002]].
- Root-cause investigations and CAPA ownership.

## Authority

May quarantine material and raise nonconformities. May not approve a procedure or close a CAPA.
