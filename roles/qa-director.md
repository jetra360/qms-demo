# QA Director

## Purpose

Accountable for the QMS as a whole and for internal audit independence.

## Responsibilities

- Owning [[POL-QUAL-001]] and approving [[POL-QUAL-002]].
- Conducting internal audits, for example [[AUDIT-2026-Q1]].
- Verifying and closing CAPAs.

## Authority

Final approval authority for policies. Sole role permitted to set `verified_by` and `closed_on` on a CAPA.

## Required training

- [[POL-QUAL-001]]
- ISO 9001:2015 internal auditor training (record held outside this demo vault).
