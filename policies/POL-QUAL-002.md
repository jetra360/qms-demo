# Data Integrity Policy

## Purpose

Records must be attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate. This policy protects the evidence on which the QMS depends.

## Scope

All paper and electronic records held in the vault, including spreadsheet registers and training records.

## Policy statements

1. Every record identifies who performed the activity and when.
2. Corrections are made with a visible audit trail; original entries are never overwritten.
3. Controlled documents are versioned in Git; the commit history is part of the record.
4. Access to sensitive registers is restricted to the roles that need them.
