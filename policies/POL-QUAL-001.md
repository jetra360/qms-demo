# Quality Policy

## Purpose

The company commits to delivering conforming product, meeting applicable statutory and customer requirements, and improving the effectiveness of the quality management system continuously. This policy sets the direction that every procedure in the vault implements.

## Scope

Applies to all processes, sites and personnel that can affect product conformity, from supplier selection through final release.

## Policy statements

1. Requirements are identified, documented and controlled before work begins.
2. Nonconforming output is isolated, evaluated and dispositioned before use or release.
3. Every controlled document has an owner, an approver and a review date.
4. Corrective actions are taken when nonconformities are found, and their effectiveness is verified.
5. Records are retained as evidence that processes were followed.
